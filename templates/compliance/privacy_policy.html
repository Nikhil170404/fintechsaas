Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 — Effective 1 January 2024

1. Data We Collect

We collect your company name, email address, and financial data you upload (client names, account numbers, transaction records). We do not collect payment card details — payments are processed directly by Razorpay.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to generate financial documents (account statements, invoices, loan schedules) and deliver them to your clients at your direction. We do not sell or share your data with third parties except integration services you explicitly connect (Zoho, Gmail, WhatsApp Business) and Razorpay for payment processing.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored in India on servers compliant with DPDP Act 2023 requirements. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Sensitive configuration (API keys, SMTP passwords) is encrypted at rest using AES-256.

4. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
  • Access: You can export all your data from Settings → Export.
  • Correction: Update your company details and client data at any time.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of all your data from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Portability: Export your data as JSON at any time.
5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active plus 30 days after deletion. Financial audit logs are retained for 7 years as required by Indian accounting regulations.

6. Cookies

We use a single session cookie for authentication. No tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Contact

Data Protection Officer: privacy@fintechdesk.app
