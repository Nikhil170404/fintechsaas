Tips & Common Questions
First time? Download the Sample Excel Template from the sidebar, fill it with your client data, then upload it here. That's it!
Excel format: Two sheets — Clients (name, email, account no) and Transactions (date, debit, credit, balance). Account numbers must match.
Before sending emails: Go to Settings → SMTP and enter your Gmail / Outlook / SMTP details. Click "Test SMTP" to confirm it works.
Your branding on PDFs: Go to Settings → Branding to upload your company logo and choose your brand color. Every PDF will use it.
GST Invoices: Select Intra-State for CGST+SGST (same state) or Inter-State for IGST (different states). The PDF splits the tax correctly.
Merge all PDFs into one: On the Clients page, click "Merge All to One PDF" to download a single combined file.
